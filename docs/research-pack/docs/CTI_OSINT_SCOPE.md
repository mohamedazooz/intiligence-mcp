# OSINT and Cyber Threat Intelligence Scope

Intended defensive and lawful workflows: public-source discovery with citations; authorized domain/asset context; indicator enrichment; STIX/TAXII exchange; approved malware/file metadata enrichment; threat actor/campaign/malware/infrastructure/vulnerability relationship research.

Out of scope: unauthorized access, credential theft, persistence, doxxing, stalking, harassment, access-control bypass, unauthorized active scanning, exfiltration/redistribution of restricted intelligence, or automated attribution based on weak indicators.

Every CTI record preserves original source, canonical reference, observation time, validity, source reliability, information credibility, confidence, analyst rationale, TLP/handling markings, redistribution constraints and whether content is observed/inferred/enriched/assessed.

OpenCTI and MISP remain separate services with separate credentials and data policies. Use documented APIs and STIX/TAXII. IntelOwl analyzers require per-analyzer license, security and egress review. ProjectDiscovery active scans require explicit target authorization and allowlisting.

High-impact attribution and claims about identifiable people require human review and evidence traceability. Automation must not silently promote hypotheses to facts.
